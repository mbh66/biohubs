## Excluded Content

- **Valley of Grace / Genadendal**: Bioregional prototype content excluded per instructions (international audience).
- **Emancipation Architecture (Movement III)**: Bioregional currency, Regenerative Participation Income, commons trusts, transvestment pathways. These are downstream applications of the consciousness thesis, not the thesis itself.
- **Alpha Window / Financial Architecture**: Movement I's financial analysis (UCC Article 8, securities architecture, CBDC analysis). These provide the urgency context but are not consciousness evolution content.
- **Coercion Continuum**: Historical slavery-to-digital-enclosure sequence. Related but belongs in a separate extraction.

## Open Decisions

1. **Steiner V1/V2 as standalone page**: The V1/V2 distinction is central to the corpus but is currently documented within `consciousness-and-value-creation.md` rather than as a separate concept page. A future extraction covering Movement II economics may want to extract it as `concepts/v1-v2-value-creation.md`.

2. **Additional source thinkers**: The corpus references Tainter, Turchin, Polanyi, Zuboff, Meadows, Margulis, Capra, Bateson, Frankl, Kimmerer, Yunkaporta, Akomolafe, Lietaer, Gesell, Ostrom, and Graeber. Source pages for these were not created in this extraction as they are referenced primarily in the Three Futures and Emancipation Architecture contexts rather than the consciousness evolution material directly. A broader extraction should add them.

3. **Spiral Dynamics color codes**: The Beige-through-Turquoise color scheme appears throughout. A glossary entry mapping all eight colors could aid readers unfamiliar with the framework. Deferred as it would require careful attribution to Beck and Cowan vs. Graves.
