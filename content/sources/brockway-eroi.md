Brockway, P. E., Owen, A., Brand-Correa, L. I., & Hardt, L. (2019). Estimation of global final-stage energy-return-on-investment for fossil fuels with comparison to renewable energy sources. *Nature Energy*.

The Brockway et al. paper is the primary contemporary measurement of energy return on investment ([[eroi|EROI]]) at the finished-fuel (rather than well-head) stage. At the finished-fuel stage the global EROI for fossil fuels is about 6 to 1 and declining, approaching what the authors call a net-energy cliff. Earlier well-head measurements had returned much higher figures (Gagnon et al. 2009 report global oil-and-gas EROI for publicly traded companies falling from 30 to 1 in 1995 to about 18 to 1 by 2006). The 2019 paper's central contribution is that the final-stage measurement is the physically relevant one, since it captures the energy actually available for economic work after processing, refining, and delivery.
