Gladek, E., et al. (2026). *BioHubs: A Pathway to Regional Resilience.* Metabolic, Anura Capital, Artistree, Basin Collective, May 2026.

The first systematic assessment of the global BioHub field. Metabolic and partners documented 152 initiatives worldwide operating under an emergent coordination logic that connects place-based ecological restoration to bioregional economic architecture. The report is the primary empirical reference for the field's current scale and composition, and it supplies the base data the Evolution of Coordination Nodes framework draws on when locating the BioHub as the +E form's concentrating node.
