Sahtouris, E. (2000). *EarthDance: Living Systems in Evolution*. iUniversity Press. ISBN: 978-0-595-13067-3.

Evolutionary biologist Elisabet Sahtouris's synthesis extending Lynn Margulis's endosymbiotic biology into a general principle: mature ecosystems are characterized by mutual aid and cooperative negotiation rather than by hostile competition. Young ecosystems (newly disturbed, resource-rich, low-diversity) are dominated by competitive opportunistic species. Mature ecosystems (complex, resource-constrained, high-diversity) are dominated by cooperative integrated networks. The trajectory of biological evolution is from hostile competition to cooperative negotiation. This is a claim about structural fitness, not about morality: cooperation is more capable than competition in complex environments. For the Emancipation Architecture, EarthDance provides the biological grounding for the Transcendence trajectory and for the Mycelial Consciousness framework.
