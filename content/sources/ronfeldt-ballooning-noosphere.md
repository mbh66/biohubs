Ronfeldt, D. (2026). *Our Planet's Ballooning Noosphere: Toward A Multi-Sphere Holonic Framework For Improving Science And Strategy — With AEONs.* Unpublished paper, July 2026. First version presented at the International Conference on AI: Noosphere and Humanities, Nanjing University, May 29-31, 2026. Forthcoming in Pan Tianqun (ed.), *The Rising of the Globe Mind: Refined Readings on the Noosphere*, Jiangsu Renmin Press, 2027.

Extends Ronfeldt's earlier work on the [[glossary/noosphere|Noosphere]] and noopolitik by proposing a tri-sphere "holonic framework" that treats the [[glossary/geosphere|Geosphere]], biosphere, and noosphere as an entangled set rather than studying any sphere in isolation. The paper draws on Arthur Koestler's concept of the holon (a whole that is simultaneously part of a larger whole) to argue that patterns recurring across all three spheres can serve as a predictive theorem: if a significant formation or process appeared during geosphere evolution and a structural-functional equivalent recurred during biosphere evolution, then something equivalent will recur during noosphere evolution.

Several arguments carry direct relevance for the BioConomy corpus. Ronfeldt critiques "superorganism" framings of the noosphere that treat humanity as a single collective organism, arguing that they risk justifying top-down control and neglecting the role of decontrol in healthy complex systems. His preferred governance form for the noosphere is the holonic network: lateral, decentralized, and nested, where each node retains autonomy while participating in larger wholes. This parallels the corpus's account of [[concepts/mycelial-coordination|Mycelial Coordination]] as an operating pattern and the [[frameworks/evolution-of-coordination-nodes|Evolution of Coordination Nodes]] from sacred sites through cathedrals and skyscrapers to BioHubs.

The paper also introduces a speculative forecast: that noosphere evolution will produce five to ten massive autonomous AI-powered entities, which Ronfeldt calls AEONs (Axial Entities of the Noosphere). These entities align around what he terms a sacred evolutionary principle: that valuing, advancing, and caring for one sphere requires caring for all three. The AEONs speculation resonates with the corpus's [[concepts/carbon-silicon-partnership|Carbon-Silicon Partnership]] and its treatment of AI as a second form of intelligence grounded in bioregional work rather than as a disembodied optimizer.

Ronfeldt frames decontrol (ceding, sharing, and distributing power and control) as a complement to control that healthy societies require. He connects this to his [[sources/ronfeldt-timn|TIMN framework]], arguing that each successive societal form (Tribes, Institutions, Markets, Networks) requires increased decontrol to function. The corpus's renaming of TIMN as TIME (Tribal, Institutional, Market, Emergent) maps the same trajectory, with the Emergent form requiring the most radical decontrol of all.

The paper references the BioConomy project directly, citing Michael Haupt's Framer OS research briefs on S-curves and [[glossary/mycelial-consciousness|Mycelial Consciousness]].
