Bauwens, M. (2026). "Peer FOR Peer: The Emerging Third Attractor." P2P Foundation, August 2026.

Bauwens's articulation of a coordination form that goes beyond peer-to-peer as a transaction pattern to peer-for-peer as a mutual-service commitment across networks of regenerative projects. The essay names the Archipelago of Regenerative Projects framing and reads the wider bioregional and regenerative movement as a converging third attractor alongside states and markets.
