INCITE! Women of Color Against Violence (2007). *The Revolution Will Not Be Funded: Beyond the Non-Profit Industrial Complex*. Cambridge, MA: South End Press. Republished 2017 by Duke University Press.

Anthology documenting how foundations act as "'cooling-out' agencies, delaying and preventing more radical, structural change." Andrea Smith recounts INCITE!'s own experience of capture: the Ford Foundation's 30 July 2004 letter reversing a $100,000 grant "because of our organization's statement of support for the Palestinian liberation struggle," triggering a "major financial crisis" that forced INCITE! to raise $60,000 in weeks. The book is the primary reference on the non-profit industrial complex thesis and, for the Three-Feature Test, on the specific mechanism through which NGO capture occurs.
