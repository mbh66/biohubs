Ruddick, W. O. (2025). *Grassroots Economics: Reflection and Practice.* Grassroots Economics Foundation.

The book-length treatment of the Commitment Pooling Protocol and Ruddick's fifteen years of field practice, from Eco-Pesa in Mombasa (2010) through the Sarafu Network (2013 to 2023) to the current [[glossary/commitment-pool|commitment pool]] phase (July 2023 onward). By the book's publication, the mechanism was operational across Kenya, Cameroon, and South Africa, covering over 80 communities and roughly 60,000 households.
