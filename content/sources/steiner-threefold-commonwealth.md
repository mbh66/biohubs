Steiner, R. (1919). *The Threefold Commonwealth* (*Die Kernpunkte der Sozialen Frage*). London: Anthroposophical Publishing (English edition; German original 1919).

Steiner's primary published work on the threefold social order: freedom in cultural life, equality in rights life, fraternity in economic life. The structural argument is that these three principles are incompatible when applied universally to a single unified system; only differentiation of the three spheres, each operating according to its own logic while cooperating with the others, makes all three simultaneously realizable. Steiner identified *liberté, égalité, fraternité* as the unconscious prophecy of what conscious threefolding would achieve. The French Revolution named the destination; two centuries later the vehicle remained unbuilt. For the Emancipation Architecture, the threefold separation is the constitutional architecture the Mycelial Patronage design implements at bioregional scale.
