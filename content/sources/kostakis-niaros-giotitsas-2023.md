Kostakis, V., Niaros, V., & Giotitsas, C. (2023). "Beyond Global Versus Local: Illuminating a Cosmolocal Framework for Convivial Technology Development." *Sustainability Science*.

The 2023 refinement of the cosmo-local production framework, extending it into a theory of convivial technology development that treats the global-versus-local dichotomy as a false choice and specifies the conditions under which globally shared knowledge and locally governed production reinforce one another over time.
