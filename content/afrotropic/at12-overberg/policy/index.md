# Policy Frameworks

Organised by jurisdiction level and domain, listing adoption status and operational depth. Coordination signals emerge when peer BioHubs share regulatory environments.

## National

| Framework | Domain | Adoption Status | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| National Water Act 36 of 1998 | Water | Adopted | Governs water allocation, ecological reserves, and catchment management |
| Mountain Catchment Areas Act 63 of 1970 | Water / Conservation | Adopted | Proclaimed catchment areas under CapeNature management |
| National Environmental Management: Biodiversity Act 10 of 2004 | Biodiversity | Adopted | Biodiversity offset and stewardship provisions |
| Companies Act 71 of 2008 | Governance | Adopted | NPC formation and governance |
| Cooperatives Act 14 of 2005 | Governance | Adopted | Primary and secondary cooperative formation |
| Communal Property Associations Act 28 of 1996 | Land | Under review | CPA formation for communal land holdings |
| Income Tax Act, Section 30 | Finance | Adopted | PBO status for NPC |

## Provincial (Western Cape)

| Framework | Domain | Adoption Status | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| Western Cape Water Supply System agreements | Water | Monitoring | Governs inter-basin transfers and dam operations |
| CapeNature management frameworks | Conservation | Adopted | RMCA management and stewardship |
