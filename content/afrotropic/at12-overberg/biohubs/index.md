The Overberg BioRegion coordinates activity through multiple BioHubs, each holding its own identity, founding cohort, services portfolio, and coordination surface. A [BioHub](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-biohub) is the unit of local organization within the BioConomy framework.

## Active BioHubs

- **[Valley of Grace](/biohubs/valley-of-grace)**: Founding BioHub, anchored at Genadendal in Ward 2 of Theewaterskloof Municipality. Finance-and-Governance-first entry into the global BioHub field. [[afrotropic/at12-vog/index|Full wiki]]

## Anticipated BioHubs

- **[Volmoed](/biohubs/volmoed)**: Anticipated coordination body in the Volmoed area.
- **[Witsand](/biohubs/witsand)**: Anticipated coordination body at the Breede estuary.

## Federation

The Overberg BioHubs are planned as a federation along the region's rivers [MS]. Each BioHub carries the [Bioregional Development Organization](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/glossary/a-e/bioregional-development-organization-bdo) functions for its own reach: the Valley of Grace for the Riviersonderend between the Theewaterskloof Dam and Swellendam, with BioHubs upstream of the dam and downstream of Swellendam holding their own reaches. The [[afrotropic/at12-overberg/charter|Overberg Charter]] will record the arrangement and the protocols between the BioHubs.

## BioHub Formation

New BioHubs form through the [BioHub Identity Template](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/templates/biohub-identity-template), a three-prompt sequence producing an Identity Statement, Field and Lineage Positioning, and Founding Compact. Contact the [[afrotropic/at12-vog/index|Valley of Grace BioHub]] for coordination on forming a new BioHub within the Overberg.
