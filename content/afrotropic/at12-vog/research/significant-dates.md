# Valley of Grace: Significant Dates

This table is a chronological summary showing how the valley made the progression through the TIMN **Form**: Tribes, Institutions, Market, Network. The evidentiary **Status** column refers to the evidence source as follows:

- **IC**: Independently Corroborated claims
- **MS**: claims resting on Moravian mission sources only (cite with attribution)
- **TBV**: claims requiring further verification

For an analytical reading of this timeline through Burgess's promise/imposition diagnostic, see [Promises to the Valley of Grace: Four Cycles from Independence to Dependence](/research/promises-to-the-valley-of-grace).

## Summary

| TIMN Form                         | Period                                                | Coordination Logic                                                                   | Characteristic Pool                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **T (Tribes)**                    | Pre-1737                                              | Kinship, loyalty, belonging                                                          | Kinship obligation: what you owe and receive determined by your relation to the group                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **+I (Institutions)**             | 1738 to 1838                                          | Hierarchical authority, written rules, fixed settlement                              | Mission economy: missionaries governed contribution (workshop labor, garden production) and mediated participation (common store, trade network, plot allocation)                                                                                                                                |
| **+MC (Market: Contributionism)** | 1806 to c. 1860                                       | Price and competitive exchange serving a producing community                         | Artisanal export economy: Herrnhut knives, furniture, carpentry traded across the colony; producers contributed skilled output and drew livelihood from the market                                                                                                                               |
| **+ME (Market:  Exploitation)**   | c. 1860 to present                                    | Price and competitive exchange serving external capital                              | Factory displacement of artisanal goods (1860s); property rights denied (1909 Act); formation apex removed (1926 college closure); apartheid enclosure (1950 onward); seasonal farm labor as terminal condition                                                                                  |
| **+E (Emerging)**                 | Early signals emerged in 2020 (with COVID) to present | Reciprocity, commons, stewardship; learning embedded in a global bioregional network | The valley as a producing community again: commitment pooling, cooperative federation, commons governance, and direct export pathways that retain value where it is produced, informed by global bioregional initiatives and by the sovereignty-of-labor-over-capital principle (Cox, Mondragon) |

## Notes on Evidentiary Status

- **IC (Independently Corroborated)**: Confirmed by multiple independent academic, archival, government, or encyclopedic sources. Safe to state as established fact.
- **MS (Mission-Sourced)**: Rests on Moravian mission archives, Kruger's *The Pear Tree Blossoms*, Balie, or the Genadendal Diaries. These sources corroborate each other because they share an origin. Cite with attribution.
- **TBV (To Be Verified)**: Plausible and consistent with surrounding evidence but requires confirmation against an independent source before publication.
- **Planned**: Future milestones in the project strategy.

## A Note on Terminology

This table was compiled using David Ronfeldt's TIMN framework and its notation: T, +I, +M, +N. In August 2026, the community-facing vocabulary was changed to TIME, replacing +N (Networks) with +E (Emergent). The renaming reflects a practical reality: the word "network" had been stretched across too many meanings to serve the coordination conversations underway in the valley. The academic and research documents in the project knowledge base retain Ronfeldt's original TIMN notation. This table retains it in all rows up to and including 2026, because the events recorded there were documented under that vocabulary. The 2038 row uses the BioHub and commitment pooling language adopted in the August 2026 shift. TIME builds on TIMN. The analytical framework is unchanged; the public-facing language has been updated to serve the people who will use it.
