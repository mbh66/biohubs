It highlighted the vulnerability of the region to soil erosion and the loss of fertile topsoil.                                                                                   |

The November floods caused damage estimated at close to **R1 billion**, with 80ha of table grapes washed away. | **Extreme erosion** occurred along many foothill rivers. The floods caused major damage to riverbanks, and it was noted that protective measures were needed to prevent **substantial loss of agricultural soil**.                                           |
| **2012**      | **Western Cape Storms (July & August)**<br>Heavy flooding and snow affected the province. In July, **rocks were dislodged on Sir Lowry's Pass**, hitting two cars and injuring 12 people. The N2 was closed.                                 | The rockfall on Sir Lowry's Pass is a direct example of the hazard. The 2012 floods also caused widespread disruption, with **many riverbanks washed away**, leading to a substantial loss of agricultural soil.                                             |
| **2016**      | **Cape Town Floods (June & July)**<br>Flooding affected between 10,000 and 15,000 people. The area was described as having a **high risk of rockfalls and mudslides** down steep mountain slopes.                                            | The risk of **rockfalls and mudslides** was explicitly highlighted. This risk was heightened by recent fires that had denuded mountains of their ground cover, increasing the potential for soil instability.                                                |
| **2018**      | **Cape Town Floods (June & August)**<br>Heavy rains affected about 4,000 homes. Bainskloof Pass was closed due to **mudslides**, and mudslides were also reported in the Hout Bay and Llandudno areas.                                       | **Mudslides** were a key impact, closing mountain passes and affecting coastal roads. The events followed a severe drought and fires, which had left slopes vulnerable to erosion.                                                                           |
| **2021**      | **Overberg Floods (May)**<br>Very heavy rainfall and strong winds caused significant damage in the Overberg.                                                                                                                                 | **Serious rill erosion** occurred, washing away large areas of crops and **topsoil**. | **2023**      | **Western Cape Floods (June & September)**<br>In June, a cold front caused around **$50 million** in damage to the agriculture sector. In September, severe floods killed at least 11 people and forced 2,710 families to evacuate.          | The September event caused **rockslides**, wash-aways, and fallen trees that damaged rail infrastructure. The October 2023 rainfall caused **slope failures and erosion** on the Franschhoek Pass.                                                           |
| **2024**      | **Western Cape Floods (July & August)**<br>Two major events occurred. The first (July 5-16) caused widespread infrastructure damage. The second (August 8-17) saw the **failure of four dams**, leading to severe flooding in Riverlands.    | The July 2024 storms caused **mountains of soil** to wash down, silting up dams and eroding farm tracks. The ground became super-saturated, and soil cover had to be replaced multiple times.                                                                |
