# Water Yield

The Valley of Grace BioHub's primary bankable service: catchment restoration in the below-dam Riviersonderend corridor to increase usable water yield for the Western Cape Water Supply System.

## Service Description

The below-dam corridor runs approximately 90 kilometres from the Theewaterskloof Dam wall to the Breede River confluence near Swellendam. This corridor currently carries no equivalent to the Greater Cape Town Water Fund's above-dam clearing programme.

Four Water Retention Landscape mechanisms deliver the yield:

1. **Alien clearing** on hillslopes and riparian zones
2. **Soil sponge restoration** through fynbos and renosterveld recovery, functioning mycorrhizal networks, and riparian corridor repair
3. **Water-efficient agriculture and agroforestry**
4. **Distributed water storage** through farm dams, restored wetlands, and on-farm water harvesting

## Evidence Base

Van Wyk (1987) documented a 55% streamflow reduction in fynbos catchments 23 years after pine infestation. [IC]

Le Maitre et al. (2019) modelled that current invasions reduce WCWSS assured yield by approximately 38 million m³/year, rising to approximately 130 million m³/year in 45 years without clearing. [IC]

Van Wilgen et al. (2008) estimated total invasive-related runoff reductions in fynbos and grassland biomes at levels significant to national water security. [IC]

## Target Financial Instrument

Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (FR31PB), JSE-listed April 2026, ZAR 2.5 billion. The bond is designed as the first in a replicable series covering South Africa's Strategic Water Source Areas. Performance payment is contingent on independently verified invasive-clearing and water-yield outcomes.

See [FR31PB Alignment](/alignments/fr31pb/) for the full alignment documentation.
