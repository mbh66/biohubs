# Ward 2 Business Forum — Mandate

**Genadendal · Greyton · Riviersonderend Corridor**

---

## Purpose

The Ward 2 Business Forum exists to grow local livelihoods by advising on and coordinating work that no single business, farm, or household can do alone. It is a voluntary forum open to everyone who lives or operates a business in Ward 2. It has no authority over any existing structure. It advises, connects, and coordinates; it does not govern, and it does not run operations.

## Where the Forum Advises

The Forum advises across six areas where coordinated effort can create paid work and bring outside income into the valley. In each area, the people and organisations doing the work are the operators. The Forum's role is to connect them, identify gaps, and help align their efforts.

### Water and Catchment

Alien clearing, soil restoration, fynbos recovery, and water-efficient farming along the Riviersonderend corridor. A listed water bond (the Cape Water Performance-Based Bond, issued by FirstRand Bank in April 2026) pays for verified water yield improvements. The Forum advises on how Ward 2 landowners, contractors, and work teams can access this programme so that restoration work becomes salaried employment, rather than volunteer work.

### Wildlife and Biodiversity 

Data and image collation of the Cape Mountain Leopard corridor, biodiversity surveys, and landowner stewardship across roughly 10,000 hectares. Corporate environmental reporting requirements create a market for verified biodiversity data. The Forum advises local teams and landowners on how to participate in data collection programmes and secure the contracts that pay for it.

### Heritage and Tourism

Genadendal is South Africa's oldest mission settlement, founded in 1738, and Greyton benefits from an established tourism economy. The Forum supports a valley-wide destination management plan that links the heritage sites, the leopard corridor, and the catchment into a single visitor experience. Tourism income stays local when local people run the services.

### Food and Markets
The Forum maintains the Valley of Grace branding and advises a Ward 2 producers' cooperative that gives smallholders, food growers, and processors a route to market. The cooperative structure means members share costs and negotiate collectively, while sharing in the profits generated.

### Skills and Employment 

The Forum is tasked with investigating the establishment of a premium high school and adult education centre that delivers training in bioregional coordination, facilitation, practical land management, and prosocial AI and robotics. The Forum treats coordination itself as a form of employment: people who organise restoration teams, run monitoring programmes, or manage market logistics are doing real work and should be paid for it.

## How It Is Funded

Two instruments anchor the Forum's economic logic. 

- The water bond (and related future financial vehicles) channels public and private capital into catchment restoration and pays for verified outcomes. 
- A community savings and lending structure (designed as a stokvel that can grow into a registered cooperative and, in time, a cooperative bank) keeps local money circulating locally.

## Who Can Join

Any Ward 2 resident, business owner, farmer, or community organisation. There is no membership fee. Participation is voluntary.

## Accountability

The Forum reports to Ward 2 residents at open quarterly meetings. Financial records are available on request. No decision that commits resources is taken without a quorate meeting.

---

*The legal form of the Forum (voluntary association, registered NPC, or ward committee substructure) is to be decided by members at the inaugural meeting.*
