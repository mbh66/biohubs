## Anchor Location

Genadendal, Ward 2, Theewaterskloof Municipality, Western Cape, South Africa. Founded 1738 as the first Moravian mission station in Southern Africa; in 1838, the site of South Africa's first teacher training college. The Learning Hub at Genadendal carries the educational heritage into 21st-century coordination forms.

## Purpose

The Valley of Grace BioHub coordinates landscape-scale activity that:

- Increases usable water yield through the four Water Retention Landscape mechanisms
- Reduces water demand in the below-dam catchment
- Maintains the Cape Mountain Leopard corridor through landowner stewardship
- Generates biodiversity data through the Guardians of Earth Nature Realm
- Anchors regenerative tourism at heritage sites and along the corridor
- Supports producers' cooperatives across Ward 2
- Runs educational programmes from the Learning Hub

Each activity stream generates value no individual actor captures alone. The BioHub is the coordination layer through which pooled commitments become bankable services.

## Entity Form

Recommended stacked entity structure (pending legal opinion):

- Non-Profit Company (NPC) as convening entity
- Primary Cooperative as producers' vehicle
- Operating company (Pty Ltd) for revenue-generating activity, mission-locked through NPC shareholding
- Trust or CPA forms where land is held

The operating logic is the federated cooperative pattern: cooperatives cooperating, jointly capitalised shared infrastructure held by the federation.

Three inherited constraints: no new financial obligations on any municipality; free and open membership; all content published under open licence unless client-confidential.
