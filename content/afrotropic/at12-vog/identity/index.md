# Identity

This section holds the three foundational identity documents produced through the [BioHub Identity Template](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/templates/biohub-identity-template).

- **[Identity Statement](/identity/identity-statement)** — Who the BioHub is: name, founding cohort, anchor location, purpose, position within the field, intellectual lineage, entity form, and forward relationship to the BioRegion.
- **[Field and Lineage Positioning](/identity/field-and-lineage-positioning)** — Comprehensive reference compiling global field mapping, cited intellectual lineage, research base, cohort publications, and specific field contribution.
- **[Founding Compact](/identity/founding-compact)** — Governance document co-signed by the founding cohort, establishing legitimacy, mutual commitments, entity form, patronage architecture, and protocols for expansion, revision, dissent, and dissolution.

**Status:** Draft, pending formal three-prompt template run with the founding cohort. Current content drawn from the Overberg BioHub Founding Brief v0.3 (August 2026).
