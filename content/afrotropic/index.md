# Afrotropic Realm

The Afrotropic realm covers Sub-Saharan Africa, Madagascar, and the western Indian Ocean islands. It holds the oldest continuous record of human habitation on Earth, the continent's major river basins, the Rift Valley lakes, the Congo rainforest, the savannas that carry the world's largest remaining megafauna populations, and the Cape Floristic Region with the highest density of plant species per unit area of any temperate region.
