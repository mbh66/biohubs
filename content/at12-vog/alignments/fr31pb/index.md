# FR31PB — Cape Water Performance-Based Bond Alignment

**Status:** Alignment in progress. Formal Bankable Service Alignment Template run not yet initiated. Current content drawn from Founding Brief v0.3 Section 6.

## Instrument Summary

The Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (JSE: FR31PB), issued by FirstRand Bank at ZAR 2.5 billion and listed 17 April 2026, is Africa's first nature-linked performance-based bond and the first outcomes-based bond by a commercial bank globally to tie investor returns to verified ecological restoration. [IC — RMB press release April 2026]

Rand Merchant Bank arranged and structured it. The Nature Conservancy South Africa is the implementation agent (ZAR 150 million for first tranche implementation). Conservation Alpha is the independent verification agent.

## Relevance to the Valley of Grace BioHub

Two features matter:

1. The bond is designed as the first in a replicable series covering South Africa's Strategic Water Source Areas.
2. Performance payment is contingent on independently verified invasive-clearing and water-yield outcomes.

The Overberg contains portions of nationally designated SWSAs [TBV against Le Maitre et al. 2018 WRC report]. The BioHub's below-dam position is distinct from the Greater Cape Town Water Fund's above-dam allocation.

## Alignment Requirements

Building toward a Valley of Grace SWSA tranche requires:

- A verification-ready restoration inventory at landscape scale
- Landowner stewardship agreements authorising the work
- A monitoring architecture aligned with Conservation Alpha's methodology
- A convening structure capable of executing at required tempo
