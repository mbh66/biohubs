# Partners

External institutional actors engaged with the Valley of Grace BioHub, whether as collaborative partners, statutory counterparties, platform providers, or historical process participants. Under the domicile convention documented in [How to Build a BioHub Wiki](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/how-to-build-a-biohub-wiki), a party belongs in Partners when its primary domicile sits outside the BioRegion. Parties domiciled inside the BioRegion sit in [Entities](/entities/) instead.

## Active Pages

- **[Urban Dynamics Western Cape (UDWC)](/partners/udwc)** — Cape Town-based town planning consultancy appointed by TWKM in November 2002 for the Farm 39 TRANCRAA transformation. Historical process participant, central to the 2007 to 2010 dispute and one of three signatories to the 7 July 2010 declaration that the Houw Hoek Agreement could not be implemented.
- **[Department of Rural Development and Land Reform (DRDLR)](/partners/drdlr)** — National government department responsible for land reform, headquartered in Pretoria. Statutory authority for TRANCRAA implementation. Merged with Agriculture in 2019 to form DALRRD, which now holds the function. Predecessor: Department of Land Affairs (DLA), signatory to the 2008 Houw Hoek Agreement.
- **[Ranyaka](/partners/ranyaka)** — National South African community development organization. Ran three structured community engagement sessions in the valley through 2024 following an initial March 2024 partnership with SACLI and church leaders.
- **[Guardians of Earth](/partners/guardians-of-earth)** — Biodiversity and stewardship platform hosting the Valley of Grace Nature Realm activated July 2026. Covers approximately 10,000 hectares; live BioScore. Digital infrastructure for the transformative tourism strategy.
