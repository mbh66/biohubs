# Ranyaka

Community development organization operating across South Africa in an integrated neighborhood transformation model that convenes residents, faith communities, business, education, and government stakeholders around a shared local plan. Ranyaka began structured engagement in the Valley of Grace in March 2024 through a partnership with SACLI (South African Christian Leadership Initiative) and local church leaders, followed by three community engagement sessions in the valley through 2024.

## Domicile and scope

Ranyaka is a national South African organization with head office outside the BioRegion the Valley of Grace BioHub anchors. Its engagement with the valley is delivered through in-person community sessions, coordinator visits, and inter-session follow-up; the organization does not maintain a domiciled local office. Under the domicile convention documented in [How to Build a BioHub Wiki](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/how-to-build-a-biohub-wiki), the page for Ranyaka sits in Partners.

## Role in the valley

The following summary draws on the [Significant Dates](/significant-dates) timeline. Every event carries the IC (Independently Corroborated) marker at source.

**Initial engagement (March 2024).** On 11 March 2024 Ranyaka met with SACLI and church leaders in the valley. This was the initial meeting that started Ranyaka's engagement process. Minutes: [11 Mar 2024 SACLI gathering](https://docs.superhuman.com/@sog/2024/11-mar-sacli-gathering-15).

**First community engagement session (April 2024).** On 18 April 2024 Ranyaka held its first community engagement session in the valley. 89 people from across the valley attended. Record: [18 Apr 2024 community engagement](https://docs.superhuman.com/@sog/2024/18-apr-ranyaka-community-engagement-12).

**Second community engagement session (June 2024).** On 13 June 2024 Ranyaka held its second community engagement session. Approximately 60 people from across the valley attended.

**Third community engagement session (July 2024).** On 24 July 2024 Ranyaka held its third community engagement session. Approximately 50 people attended. Record: [24 Jul 2024 community engagement](https://docs.superhuman.com/@sog/2024/24-jul-ranyaka-community-engagement-9).

**2025 to 2026.** Continuation of the engagement process into 2025 and 2026 is not documented in the current wiki record. Ranyaka's role in the Valley of Grace BioHub's founding cohort and its position on the coordination surface are flagged for confirmation as the BioHub's partner relationships are formalized.

## Position relative to the BioHub

Ranyaka's community engagement work in the valley predates the formal launch of the Valley of Grace BioHub (September 2026) by more than two years. The three 2024 sessions established a community coordination surface the BioHub can now build on, though the relationship between Ranyaka's engagement architecture and the BioHub's coordination surface has not been documented in the wiki. Whether Ranyaka becomes a founding partner of the BioHub, a delivery partner on specific services (education, community development, coordination-as-employment), or a peer coordinator on a distinct track is an open question for the [Coordination Surface](/coordination-surface) and the [Founding Compact](/identity/founding-compact).
