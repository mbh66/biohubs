# Tenderable Services Portfolio

The BioHub's services are the coordination outputs that external counterparties can contract with. Each service page carries structured frontmatter using the [controlled vocabulary](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/glossary/) for AI agent interoperability.

## Services

- **[Water Yield](/services/water-yield)** — Catchment restoration through Water Retention Landscape methodology
- **[Biodiversity Data](/services/biodiversity-data)** — Leopard corridor monitoring and Nature Realm data
- **[Heritage and Tourism](/services/heritage-and-tourism)** — Leopard Corridor DMP and Genadendal Learning Hub
- **[Food Systems](/services/food-systems)** — Producers' cooperative and federated supply chain
- **[Carbon Sequestration](/services/carbon-sequestration)** — Soil carbon through fynbos and renosterveld restoration
- **[Coordination as Employment](/services/coordination-as-employment)** — Learning Hub delivery, diagnostic facilitation, BioHub coordination

**Note:** Service pages to be populated from the Value Proposition Template outputs. Draft content drawn from the Founding Brief v0.3 Sections 5-6.
