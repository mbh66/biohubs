# Founding Compact

**Status:** Placeholder. This document will be produced through the BioHub Identity Template Prompt 3 with the founding cohort, drawing on the governance framework outlined in the Founding Brief v0.3 Section 8.

The Founding Compact is a governance document co-signed by the founding cohort. It establishes the BioHub as a coordination body worthy of commitment and serves as constitutional guidance.

## Anticipated Content

Based on the [BioHub Identity Template](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/templates/biohub-identity-template), the Compact will address:

- Founding cohort signatories and their roles
- Entity form (the NPC-cooperative-operating company stack)
- Patronage architecture (Mycelial Patronage and FR31PB bond channels)
- Decision-making framework
- Cohort expansion protocols
- Revision and amendment procedures
- Dissent and minority-position protocols
- Dissolution conditions
- Multi-BioHub coordination protocols (provisional, pending BioRegion Establishment Template)

## Inherited Constraints

Three constraints from the founding period govern the design:

1. No new financial obligations may be placed on any municipality in the district.
2. Membership is free and open.
3. All content produced by the BioHub is published under an open licence unless client-confidential.
