Public knowledge commons whose scope overlaps the BioConomy corpus. Each entry says what the wiki covers, who runs it, where its treatment goes deeper than this one, and where the intellectual traffic between the two projects flows.

## Overview

The BioConomy corpus stands inside a network of aligned wikis. Several long-running knowledge commons treat concepts this wiki also uses (peer production, cosmolocalism, commons governance, bioregional economics, cooperative federation), often at greater depth on their own terrain. Pointing to them explicitly saves this wiki from duplicating work that has already been done well, and it lets a reader follow a thread out of the BioConomy frame into an adjacent one when the question calls for it.

## Currently listed

- [[p2p-foundation-wiki|P2P Foundation Wiki]] (wiki.p2pfoundation.net). [[michel-bauwens|Michel Bauwens]]'s long-running knowledge commons for peer production, open cooperativism, commons governance, and cosmolocalism.
- [[omniharmonic-wiki|Omniharmonic]] (wiki.omniharmonic.com). [[benjamin-life|Benjamin Life]]'s Quartz-based research wiki on bioregional governance, regenerative accelerationism, and the meta-crisis.

## How the folder is organized

One page per external wiki. Each entry sits under a short slug (the wiki's common name) and carries an overview, the editorial and organizational context, the topical territory where its coverage is strongest, and the specific BioConomy pages that already lean on its material or should. Where the wiki's founder or a principal contributor has a page under `people/`, the entry links to it.
