British-Pakistani systems analyst and investigative journalist whose Planetary Phase Shift framework provides the peer-reviewed synthesis integrating Holling's adaptive cycle with phase transition theory across the five foundational sectors of civilization's production system (energy, transport, food, information, materials).

## Contribution

Ahmed's central finding, published in *Foresight* (2025, 27(2)): all five foundational sectors of civilization's production system are simultaneously entering phase transition. This is not a crisis in one sector creating ripple effects in others. It is the simultaneous destabilization of the entire material foundation on which the current organizing system rests.

The framework corrects Arbib and Seba's technology disruption analysis for its colonial blind spots and integrates it with Gunderson and Holling's [[adaptive-cycle|adaptive cycle]] theory. Multiple global crises are, in this reading, symptoms of the final stages of industrial civilization's life-cycle.

For the [[frameworks/three-futures|Three Futures]] framework, Ahmed's work provides the peer-reviewed empirical grounding for the [[frameworks/three-futures|Termination]] trajectory: the diagnosis of what happens if the current system does not reorganize at a lower level of complexity.
