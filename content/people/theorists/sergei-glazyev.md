Russian economist, member of the Russian Academy of Sciences, adviser to President Putin from 2012, Minister for Integration and Macroeconomics of the Eurasian Economic Commission, and, per the source brief, State Secretary of the Union State of Russia and Belarus from 2025. Glazyev's published work critiques dollar hegemony and advocates a new monetary architecture organized around Eurasian integration, local-currency settlement between Russia and China, and a proposed commodity-backed settlement unit for BRICS+.

Glazyev has repeatedly and publicly acknowledged intellectual debt to Lyndon LaRouche, whom he invited to give keynote testimony at June 2001 Duma hearings on world financial collapse, and whom he eulogized on the centenary of LaRouche's birth (September 2022) as "the deepest thinker of our time." This wiki notes the transmission line without adopting LaRouche's own conspiratorial framing. The relevant Hamilton, Carey, and List materials in the LaRouche circulation are documented independently, and the network is used only where independent verification exists.

Glazyev belongs on this list as a transmission point where the Hamilton-Carey-List [[glossary/retention-economics|retention]] lineage re-enters state policy at the level of a major power. The mechanisms he advocates (monetary sovereignty, physical-[[glossary/economy|economy]] investment over financial speculation, commodity-backed settlement) are recognizably the same family as Lincoln's greenbacks and Mattei's sovereign energy strategy, ported to the twenty-first century. Whether Glazyev's proposals move from policy influence to institutional realization is a live question the thesis tracks alongside the Sahel wave.
