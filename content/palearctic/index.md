# Palearctic Realm

The Palearctic realm covers Europe, North Africa, and northern and central Asia. It is the largest biogeographic realm by land area, stretching from Iceland and the Azores to Kamchatka and Japan. It holds the Siberian taiga (the largest terrestrial biome), the steppes of Central Asia, the Mediterranean basin, the Alps, the Himalayas' northern slopes, and the boreal and temperate forests that shaped European settlement patterns. The realm carries the longest continuous record of institutional land governance, from Roman cadastral systems through medieval commons to modern protected-area networks.

## BioRegions

No BioRegions have been formalised within the Palearctic realm. As communities begin coordinating through the [BioConomy framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-bioconomy), their BioRegions and BioHubs will be registered here.
