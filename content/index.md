This wiki documents the [[bioconomy|BioConomy]] framework: a coordination architecture for economies organized around [[bioregion|BioRegions]]. It exists to help those navigating the liminal space that inevitably occurs during periods of societal transition.

## How to engage with the wiki

If you're new to this movement, read these three orientation essays first. Together they place you inside the framework the rest of the wiki adopts.

- [[essays/what-is-a-biohub|What Is a BioHub?]]
- [[essays/what-is-a-bioregion|What Is a BioRegion?]]
- [[essays/what-is-a-bioconomy|What Is a BioConomy?]] (and how it differs from the Economy)

If you're already familiar with this vocabulary and want to run the framework in your own bioregion, the four-template [[templates/index|founding suite]] is where the wiki becomes operational. [[essays/how-to-engage-your-bioregion|How to Engage Your Bioregion]] helps you cross the bridge from theory to practice.

Use the navigation on your left to jump directly into whatever area of interest you have.
