# Neotropic Realm

The Neotropic realm covers Central and South America and the Caribbean. It holds the Amazon basin (the largest tropical rainforest and river system on Earth), the Andes (the longest continental mountain range), the Cerrado, the Pantanal, the Atlantic Forest, the Galápagos, and the coral systems of the Caribbean. The realm carries the highest species richness of any biogeographic realm for birds, amphibians, and vascular plants.

## BioRegions

No BioRegions have been formalised within the Neotropic realm. As communities begin coordinating through the [BioConomy framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-bioconomy), their BioRegions and BioHubs will be registered here.
