# Nearctic Realm

The Nearctic realm covers North America north of central Mexico, from the Arctic tundra through the boreal forests, the Great Plains, the temperate deciduous forests of the east, and the deserts and mountain systems of the west. It holds the Great Lakes (the largest surface freshwater system on Earth), the Mississippi-Missouri watershed, old-growth temperate rainforests of the Pacific Northwest, and grassland ecosystems that once supported the largest ungulate herds in the Western Hemisphere.

## BioRegions

No BioRegions have been formalised within the Nearctic realm. As communities begin coordinating through the [BioConomy framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-bioconomy), their BioRegions and BioHubs will be registered here.
