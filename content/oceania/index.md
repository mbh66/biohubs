# Oceania Realm

The Oceania realm covers the Pacific islands and Hawai'i, spanning Melanesia, Micronesia, and Polynesia. It holds the most geographically dispersed land areas of any realm, the highest rates of endemism per unit area on Earth, coral atolls and volcanic archipelagos, and Indigenous navigation and resource-management traditions that sustained long-distance oceanic societies for centuries. The realm's small land areas and extreme isolation make its ecosystems among the most vulnerable to invasive species and sea-level change.

## BioRegions

No BioRegions have been formalised within the Oceania realm. As communities begin coordinating through the [BioConomy framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-bioconomy), their BioRegions and BioHubs will be registered here.
