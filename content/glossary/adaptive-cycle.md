C. S. Holling's four-phase model of the dynamics of complex adaptive systems: growth (r), conservation (K), release (Omega), and reorganization (alpha). Systems cycle through these phases as they mature, accumulate structure, undergo disturbance, and reorganize. The cycle is scale-invariant: the same pattern is observable in forests, in economies, in civilizations. In the Planetary Phase Shift framework, industrial civilization is entering the Omega (release) and alpha (reorganization) phases simultaneously across all five foundational sectors.
