The coordination and commitment document co-signed by a [[biohub|BioHub]]'s cohort that establishes what the cohort commits to build toward readiness for a specific financial instrument. Produced by Prompt 3 of the [[templates/bankable-service-alignment-template|Bankable Service Alignment Template]].

## Extended definition

An [[alignment-statement|Alignment Statement]] maps what the BioHub already delivers against what the instrument already requires. The gap between them is what the Alignment Compact is for. It specifies the build-out: monitoring and verification architecture to be constructed, governance requirements to be met, partnerships to be formalized, resource requirements and funding pathway, risk register, and timeline.

The Compact is a [[e-form-emergent|+E coordination instrument]]. It binds the cohort to a shared build-out trajectory without depending on any single institution's mandate or any single funder's cycle. The commitment is to the coordination work itself, governed by the decision-making forms established in the [[founding-compact|Founding Compact]].

Length is five to ten pages. Co-signed by the cohort. Used internally to coordinate the build-out work, and externally to demonstrate to instrument parties that a credible build-out plan is underway.

## Contrast with adjacent instruments

The [[tender-compact|Tender Compact]] establishes what the BioHub commits to tender across its portfolio. The Alignment Compact establishes what the cohort commits to build to reach readiness for one specific instrument. Once the build-out is complete, the specific tender operates under the Tender Compact and the Alignment Compact's commitments are either fulfilled or carried forward into operational work.

A project plan enumerates tasks and their timing. The Alignment Compact enumerates commitments and their dependencies. The project plan is a management artifact; the Compact is a coordination artifact co-signed by those who hold the commitments.

## Structural elements

1. Preamble stating what the cohort commits to build and why.
2. Build-out commitments.
3. Monitoring and verification architecture.
4. Governance requirements.
5. Partnership commitments.
6. Timeline (milestone-based, with dependencies).
7. Resource requirements and funding pathway.
8. Risk register.
9. Review and revision cycle.
10. Relationship to the Tender Compact.
11. Co-signatures section.
