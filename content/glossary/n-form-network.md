Coordination through all-channel connection at planetary scale, where distributed nodes exchange information, goods, and services across distance without a central controller. Its material substrate is the global connective infrastructure built through the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries: containerized supply chains, undersea cables, the internet, and low-earth-orbit satellite constellations.

## Usage in the framework

The +N form is the fourth of Ronfeldt's original TIMN sequence and the one currently mid-transition. Its early promise (near-free global connectivity through the internet and satellite networks like Iridium and Starlink) was largely kept. Its late trajectory is capture by +M logic into surveillance capitalism, and Ronfeldt's own 2025 reassessment notes that +N is not favoring any of the earlier three realms. The BioConomy corpus follows Ronfeldt's proposed TIMN-to-[[time|TIME]] move, replacing +N with [[e-form-emergent|+E (Emergent)]] to name the coordination form that survives when the fiat and market substrate is removed. On this reading, +N is the connective substrate; +E is the coordination form that runs on it without collapsing back into +M.
