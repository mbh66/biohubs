The fifth Cognitive Major Evolutionary Transition: the shift from Material to Mycelial modes of perception, cognition, and coordination that the current civilizational transition is calling for. The capacity to hold multiple perspectives simultaneously without collapsing into any single one, and to collaborate across formerly competing worldviews. The psychological substrate that Clare Graves called Second Tier thinking.
