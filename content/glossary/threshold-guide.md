The role identifier used in Michael Haupt's Framer OS body of work for a person who accompanies others across the threshold from Material to Mycelial Consciousness. Not a teacher, not a coach, not a therapist: a Threshold Guide is a companion for the specific transition the [[frameworks/three-futures|Three Futures]] framework identifies as required for civilizational Transcendence.

The role is developed across the coordinator's Framer OS essays and is presented as a working hypothesis. It sits alongside the [[framer|Framer]] as one of the emerging role forms the Mycelial Consciousness transition requires.
