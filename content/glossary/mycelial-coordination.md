The operating pattern of the [[e-form-emergent|Emergent (E) form]] in the TIME framework: decentralized, bioregional, adaptive coordination organized around whole-system health rather than around competition or extraction. Named for the fungal mycelial networks that connect trees in a mature forest, distributing resources according to local need without a central regulator.
