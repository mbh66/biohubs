# Indomalayan Realm

The Indomalayan realm covers South and Southeast Asia and southern China, from the Indian subcontinent through the Malay Peninsula to the islands west of the Wallace Line. It holds the Western Ghats, the Sundarbans, the forests of Borneo and Sumatra, the Mekong basin, and rice-paddy landscapes that have supported dense human settlement for millennia. The realm contains some of the highest levels of terrestrial biodiversity on Earth alongside some of the highest population densities.

## BioRegions

No BioRegions have been formalised within the Indomalayan realm. As communities begin coordinating through the [BioConomy framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-bioconomy), their BioRegions and BioHubs will be registered here.
