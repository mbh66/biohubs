# Australasia Realm

The Australasia realm covers Australia, New Guinea, New Zealand, and the islands of eastern Indonesia east of the Wallace Line. It holds the world's oldest continuous land surfaces, the Great Barrier Reef, the tropical rainforests of northern Queensland and New Guinea, the eucalypt woodlands, and Indigenous land management traditions spanning at least 65,000 years. New Zealand's isolation produced a flora and fauna with no close parallels elsewhere.

## BioRegions

No BioRegions have been formalised within the Australasia realm. As communities begin coordinating through the [BioConomy framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-bioconomy), their BioRegions and BioHubs will be registered here.
