# Coordination Surface

This page describes what the Overberg BioRegion collectively offers and seeks. For BioHub-specific offers and seeks, see each BioHub's own coordination surface.

## Regional Offers

The Overberg's collective offer is landscape-scale coordination across the below-dam Riviersonderend corridor, producing verified water yield, biodiversity data, and heritage-anchored regenerative tourism. Individual BioHubs tender specific services; the BioRegion holds the shared ecological infrastructure, evidence base, and policy environment that makes those tenders credible.

Currently, the [Valley of Grace BioHub](/biohubs/valley-of-grace) is the founding and only operational BioHub. Regional offers will expand as additional BioHubs form.

## Regional Seeks

**Verification methodology alignment.** The FR31PB bond programme's verification agent (Conservation Alpha) uses water-yield outcome methodology. The BioRegion needs alignment across all BioHubs tendering into the programme.

**Landowner stewardship.** Corridor-scale restoration requires stewardship agreements that cross BioHub boundaries.

**BioHub formation.** Coordinators and founding cohorts for anticipated BioHubs at Volmoed and Witsand.

**Peer BioRegion coordination.** Shared methodology with peer BioRegions operating comparable catchment contexts in other Strategic Water Source Areas.

## Shared Financial Instruments

**Cape Water Performance-Based Bond (FR31PB).** The bond is designed as the first in a replicable series covering South Africa's Strategic Water Source Areas. Individual BioHubs tender into this programme; the BioRegion holds the shared catchment evidence and the multi-BioHub coordination protocols that make a regional tranche fundable.
