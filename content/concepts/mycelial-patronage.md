Mycelial Patronage is the form of patronage that carries the three features of every durable historical patronage case (constitutive investment, embedding in the emerging order, a replication mechanism that survives the founder) while dissolving the hierarchical structure that every prior case shared.

## Overview

Historical patronage has produced two kinds of outcomes. The rare successes (the Benedictine network, the Cistercian order, the Medici Platonic revival, the Nizamiyya madrasa network, Charlemagne's literate administration, the Jesuits, the Hanseatic League) built architectures that survived their founders and reshaped civilizational trajectories. The many failures (Roger Bacon, Francis Bacon, the Fugger banking house, the EA/FTX network, captured NGOs, the late Roman senatorial aristocracy) produced brilliant documents, decorative philanthropy, or absorbed reform organizations, and left no durable substrate.

The [[frameworks/three-feature-test|Three-Feature Test]] identifies what separates the two: constitutive investment (the patron's standing depends on the reform succeeding), embedding in the emerging order (the patron's assets appreciate as the new order consolidates), and protection during maximum exposure (the patron can shield the reform when it draws organized opposition). Every durable case has all three; every failure is missing at least one.

Mycelial Patronage inherits the three features but changes the structural relationship between patron and reform. In every historical case, the relationship was hierarchical: a patron above, a project below, capital flowing downward. The Medici owned the villa, directed Ficino, and controlled the allocation. The Carolingian patron mandated the schools from above. The Cistercian General Chapter legislated from a founding house. Even the durable architectures retained the pattern of concentrated authority at the top.

In Mycelial Patronage, the patron's capital enters the substrate and becomes the substrate. The [[glossary/commons|commons]] trust holds it in perpetuity. The bioregional currency circulates it. The clearing layer connects it to other bioregions. The patron participates as a community member with standing earned through contribution, not through capital control.

## The three features carried through

**Constitutive investment.** A wealth holder who transfers land into a Bioregional Commons Trust does not retain a board seat proportional to capital. Their authority in the bioregional order becomes a function of their participation in the community the commons serves, not of their ownership of it. This is constitutive in the strict sense the criteria define. It is also, structurally, further than the Medici went: the patron cannot pull the capital back into the old regime. The crossing is one-directional by design.

**Embedding in the emerging order.** The [[frameworks/five-transvestment-pathways|five transvestment pathways]] are the mechanism by which the patron's assets migrate from the declining regime into the emerging one. Pathway A places land into a commons trust. Pathway B capitalizes the currency reserve of a Bioregional Financing Facility. Pathway C endows [[glossary/regenerative|regenerative]] enterprises through interest-free loans repaid in bioregional currency. Pathway D converts inheritance into commons endowment. Pathway E is the demurrage dividend, in which the mere holding of bioregional currency contributes to the commons through its [[glossary/demurrage|carrying cost]]. Each pathway progressively deepens the patron's embedding in the substrate they helped bring into existence.

**Replication mechanism.** The EA replicates through bioregional nodes, each specific to its watershed and ecology, connected through an inter-bioregional clearing protocol. The critical difference from every historical precedent: the replication mechanism is the substrate itself (currency, trust, clearing layer), not a document, a constitution, or a founding house. The "code" is alive in the economic activity of every participating [[glossary/bioregion|bioregion]]. This is the structural analogue to the Rule of St Benedict (portable code, no load-bearing central node) and the Cistercian Charter of Charity (local autonomy within translocal standards), but the code lives in the money, not in the manuscript.

## How the hierarchy dissolves

Three design features dissolve the hierarchical structure every prior patronage form shared:

**Governance separation.** The EA applies Steiner's [[threefolding|threefold separation]]: economic function governed by competence assessed through associative processes; rights function governed by democratic participation; cultural function governed by individual initiative recognized by the community. No single actor controls all three domains, including the transvestor.

**Constellational architecture.** The institutional design is a [[glossary/constellation|constellation]] of many entity types across three nested scales (bioregional, inter-bioregional, and the mycelial network layer). One institution can be captured. A constellation remains resilient. The design goal is that no single actor can capture the whole, because there is no whole to capture.

**Demurrage as structural distribution.** Carrying cost on held currency ensures that value circulates toward productive use. Capital flows toward need because standing still costs more than moving. This is the monetary expression of how carbon moves through Suzanne Simard's forest floor: surplus flows to need through the network's own design, with no central allocator directing the flow.

## Contrast with adjacent forms

Mycelial Patronage is not [[regenerative-capitalism|regenerative capitalism]]. Regenerative capitalism retains the private ownership form and asks it to serve regeneration. Mycelial Patronage transfers ownership itself into a commons form.

It is not impact investment. Impact investment retains the option of exit. Mycelial Patronage forecloses exit by design.

It is not [[transvestment|transvestment alone]]. Transvestment names the crossing; Mycelial Patronage is the destination the crossing arrives at.

It is not philanthropy. Philanthropy is retractable on the funder's schedule and accountable to the funder's metrics. Mycelial Patronage is inalienable and accountable to the commons the capital has entered.

The Patagonia transfer to the Patagonia Purpose Trust and the Holdfast Collective in 2022 is the closest contemporary precedent. The Bosch and Zeiss/Carl-Zeiss-Stiftung models are historical analogues. The EA's architecture extends the pattern by adding the currency, commons trust, and clearing infrastructure that Patagonia's single-firm transfer did not.

## The unresolved parameter

The EA's target scale (heterogeneous, non-voluntary, multi-million-person bioregional networks) exceeds every historical precedent for multi-scale coherence. The Hansa achieved coherence across genuine heterogeneity through shared commercial interest and monopoly rents. The Jesuits achieved global coherence through formation and obedience within a self-selected religious order. Neither matches the EA's target conditions. The historical record supports multi-scale coherence in principle; it provides no clean precedent at the scale the bioregional transition targets.

This is the largest unproven parameter of the framework. The Valley of Grace prototype in South Africa is one live test at bioregional scale. Others are emerging. The structural argument does not depend on any single prototype succeeding; it depends on the engineering specification being correct. If a prototype fails and the specification is correct, another bioregion will follow. If the specification is wrong, the failure will be the warrant for revising it.
