# Antarctica Realm

The Antarctica realm covers the Antarctic continent and the subantarctic islands. It holds the planet's largest ice sheet, the Southern Ocean's circumpolar current system, krill populations that anchor the southern marine food web, and research stations governed under the Antarctic Treaty System (1959). Subantarctic islands carry endemic species assemblages shaped by extreme isolation.

## BioRegions

No BioRegions have been formalised within the Antarctica realm. As communities and research bodies begin coordinating through the [BioConomy framework](https://wiki.bioconomy.earth/essays/what-is-a-bioconomy), their BioRegions and BioHubs will be registered here.
